# Product

## Register

product

## Users

NoMoreVibe serves people evaluating AI-built products, makers maintaining an evidence-backed public
profile, and operators reviewing automated collection. Visitors need to understand what a product
does, whether it is active, and which claims come from the maker versus an independent public
source. Makers need low-friction registration and precise control without turning the public page
into an unverified advertisement.

## Product Purpose

NoMoreVibe is an evidence-first discovery database for products built with AI. It shows qualified
interest sent through NoMoreVibe, current public-source facts, update history, and optional build
provenance without collapsing them into an opaque score. Success means a visitor can judge a
product's identity, activity, reach, and evidence quality from one readable profile while every
claim keeps its source and freshness state.

## Brand Personality

Precise, candid, calm. The interface should feel like a well-maintained reference tool: direct
about uncertainty, restrained in presentation, and confident because it shows evidence instead of
marketing superlatives.

## Anti-references

- A Product Hunt clone that reduces discovery to launch-day popularity or unexplained points.
- A stats-only dashboard that makes raw numbers look like total product traffic.
- Dark, glassy, gradient-heavy SaaS chrome that competes with the product evidence.
- Tiny metadata, decorative timeline rails, nested cards, or badges whose meaning depends on color
  alone.
- Maker claims presented as independently verified facts.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the source: maker declarations and observed facts remain visibly distinct.
2. Explain uncertainty: collecting, stale, disconnected, conflicting, and unavailable are different
   states and must not collapse into zero.
3. Put judgment before decoration: identity, qualified interest, activity, and evidence appear before
   long histories or promotional copy.
4. Keep operation reversible and auditable: automated observations retain last-known-good values and
   moderation changes leave history.
5. Use familiar product affordances and a restrained visual system so dense evidence remains easy to
   scan.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All public and administrative UI targets WCAG AA for normal text. Visible text is never smaller than
13 px; long product prose is 15 px and structured/update copy is 14 px. Keyboard focus must be
visible, primary controls must be at least 44 px high, status cannot rely on color alone, images need
descriptive alternative text, motion respects reduced-motion preferences, and supported mobile
views must not create document-level horizontal scrolling.
